(Owner accepted).

- UserAccountRepository.findActiveByParty resolves a Person's account
  through the Party's Golden Record and the account's membership in the
  Party's tenant, so a global account that originated in another tenant
  is found. The membership only has to exist: ACTIVE stays the caller's
  decision. An origin account without a membership row still resolves in
  its own tenant, and user_accounts.party_id is never the key.
- findActiveById looks an account up by its own key, for what an account
  does on itself. UserAccountProfileRecord carries mdm_party_id, so
  callers derive the Party of their tenant.
- JdbcPartyAccessQueryAdapter uses the same derivation for the Team and
  console read models.
- grantOrganizationScopedOwnerRoleByPlatformAdmin records the Platform
  Admin's global account in assigned_by_user_id and created_by_user_id,
  next to granted_by_platform_admin_id; actor keys are global since
  Gystigo V58. Nothing is invented when the grant names no account.
